The Platform

Built around the learner

Every module designed to keep students progressing — and to keep teachers and parents in the picture.

Video Lessons

Teachers record or upload lessons per topic; students pause, rewind and revisit any time before exams.

Timed E-Exams

Countdown timer, auto-submit at zero, instant objective marking, theory scripts routed to teachers.

Progress Tracking

Per-subject progress rings show students exactly where they stand — and what to revise next.

Digital Library

PDF notes, past questions and revision packs organised by class, subject and term — downloadable anytime.

Teacher Feedback

Marked assignments return with comments; students ask questions directly under each lesson.

Smart Notifications

Exam timetables, new materials, fee reminders and results — pushed to the right people at the right time.

Examination Centre

Serious exams, zero paper

1 · Prepare

Teachers build question banks per subject and topic — multiple choice and theory — then schedule the exam per class.

2 · Write

Students sign in, the countdown starts, one question per screen, answers saved as they go, auto-submit at zero.

3 · Release

Objectives mark instantly; teachers mark theory online; the administrator approves before results publish.
